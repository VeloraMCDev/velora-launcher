repository with no repository-wide MIT or other open-source license grant.

them; they do not grant rights to gameplay, all artwork,
trademarks, bundled third-party code or historical binary distributions.

restriction; it does not relicense gameplay or grant trademark/binary distribution

source or artifacts. Third-party licenses and notices remain in effect.
